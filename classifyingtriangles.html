🔺 Classifying Triangles

Geometry Unit: Triangles

🎯 Learning Target

I can classify triangles by their sides and by their angles.

📖 Lesson Notes

Triangles can be classified in two different ways: by sides and by angles.

By Sides

  • Equilateral = 3 congruent sides
  • Isosceles = at least 2 congruent sides
  • Scalene = no congruent sides

By Angles

  • Acute = all angles less than 90°
  • Right = one angle exactly 90°
  • Obtuse = one angle greater than 90°

A triangle gets one classification from each category. A triangle can be both isosceles and right.
