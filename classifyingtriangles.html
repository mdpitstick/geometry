🔺 Classifying Triangles

Geometry Unit: Triangles

🎯 Learning Target

I can classify triangles by their sides and by their angles.

📖 Lesson Notes

Triangles can be classified in two different ways: by their sides and by their angles.

Classifying by Sides

  • Equilateral: 3 congruent sides
  • Isosceles: At least 2 congruent sides
  • Scalene: No congruent sides

Classifying by Angles

  • Acute: All angles are less than 90°
  • Right: One angle is exactly 90°
  • Obtuse: One angle is greater than 90°

A triangle can have one classification by sides and one classification by angles. For example, a triangle can be both isosceles and right.
