Triangle Angle Sum Theorem & Exterior Angle Theorem

🎯 Learning Target

I can use the Triangle Angle Sum Theorem and the Exterior Angle Theorem to find missing angle measures in triangles.

📖 Lesson Notes

Triangles have special angle relationships that help us solve for missing angle measures.

🔺 Triangle Angle Sum Theorem

The sum of the measures of the interior angles of any triangle is always 180°.

m∠A + m∠B + m∠C = 180°

To find a missing interior angle, subtract the known angles from 180°.

Example:
55° + 70° + x = 180°
125° + x = 180°
x = 55°

➡️ Exterior Angle Theorem

An exterior angle is created when one side of a triangle is extended.

The measure of an exterior angle equals the sum of the two remote interior angles.

Exterior Angle = Remote Interior Angle + Remote Interior Angle

Example:
50° + 65° = 115°

💡 Quick Tips

  • Interior angles always add to 180°.
  • Exterior angles use addition of remote interior angles.
  • Look carefully for interior versus exterior angles.
  • Draw extra marks or labels if the diagram looks confusing.
  • Always check that your answer makes sense.
